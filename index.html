Frequently Asked Questions

Know about arriving, staying, and flowing with us at the Nilgiris.

Why Flowfest 2.0? ›
If you have spent months carrying the constant mental tabs, rush, and subtle loneliness of city routine, you do not need another chaotic weekend where you return more tired than when you left. Flowfest is a 10-day mountain sanctuary to help you lay down that weight, breathe deeply, and reconnect with your natural creative rhythm. You leave with a restored nervous system and a circle where you genuinely feel you belong.
Where is Flowfest 2.0 held and how do I reach? ›
Flowfest 2.0 is hosted at The Door to Nirvana Backpackers Hostel in Kotagiri—a community property rated 4.8★ on Google and verified across Booking.com and Hostelworld. Kotagiri is well connected by direct bus service from Bengaluru, Chennai, Ooty, and Coonoor. The nearest airport is Coimbatore (CJB), and the nearest major railway junction is Mettupalayam (MTP) / Coimbatore (CBE).
Is Flowfest a crowded commercial festival? ›
No. Flowfest is an intentional, conscious mountain retreat. To preserve depth and intimacy, Flowfest is capped at 35 participants only. There are no loud commercial festival stages. Facilitators and participants live and co-create together as an intimate circle rooted in art, music, dance, meditation, and stillness.
Do I have to attend all 10 days? ›
While the full journey runs from December 24 to January 3, flexible check-in windows (with minimum 2 day passes and full 10-day passes) are available subject to room and bed availability.
What is included in the retreat pass? ›
Every pass includes chosen accommodation (dormitory or private rooms), complete access to all workshops, circles, live performances, and guided nature hikes. We also have food options available at the property which can be pre-booked at extra charges.
How does the invite application work? ›
Please click the Reserve Invite button below to lock your Early Bird pass with a ₹500 seat-allocation token (100% refundable until November 30, 2026 and fully credited toward your final pass). Our team will contact you regarding the onboarding process soon.
