CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about arriving, staying, and flowing with us at Kotagiri.

Where is Flowfest held and how do I reach? ›
Flowfest takes place at The Door to Nirvana Backpackers Hostel in Kotagiri, Nilgiris. The nearest airport is Coimbatore (CJB), and the nearest major railway junction is Mettupalayam (MTP) / Coimbatore (CBE). Scenic shared cabs and buses take you right up the tea estate hills into Kotagiri.
Do I have to attend all 10 days? ›
While the full journey runs from December 24 to January 3, flexible check-in windows (including 4-day, 6-day, and full 10-day passes) are available subject to room and bed availability.
What is included in the retreat pass? ›
Every pass includes chosen accommodation (dormitory or private rooms), all wholesome farm-to-table meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner), and complete access to all workshops, circles, live performances, and guided nature hikes.
Is Flowfest a commercial festival or a retreat? ›
Flowfest is an intentional, conscious mountain retreat. There are no loud commercial festival stages. Facilitators and participants live and co-create together as an intimate circle rooted in art, music, dance, meditation, and stillness.
How does the invite application work? ›
To preserve depth and intimacy, Flowfest is capped at 35 participants. After you submit your application form, our hosting circle reviews it and connects with you via WhatsApp for a brief alignment conversation before confirming your booking.
